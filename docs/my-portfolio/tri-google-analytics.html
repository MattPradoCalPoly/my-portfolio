Behind the Scenes with Google Analytics

TRI Website Analysis — IBM 6100

Author

Matthew Prado, Saurabh Parate, Bhamini Pillai, Bansi Khachar & Lucynda Young

Published

December 31, 2023

Project Overview

A data analytics project analyzing real user behavior and website performance for TRI (Trauma Resource Institute).

  • Data sources: Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Squarespace
  • Timeframe: February – April (3 months)
  • Goal: Identify trends in user behavior, acquisition, demographics, and landing page performance

Tools & Data Sources

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Squarespace
  • Hotjar (recommended)
  • Google Optimize (recommended for A/B testing)

Acquisition Overview

Metric Value
Active Users 2,300
New Users 2,100
Returning Users 582
Retention Rate 27.7%
Most Active Days April 23–24 (57 users)
  • Higher engagement in the middle to end of each month
  • Lower engagement at the beginning of each month

Traffic Sources

Channel Sessions
Organic Search 2,200
Direct 1,500
Referral 208
Organic Social 84
Paid Search 34
Email 10
  • Organic Search & Direct traffic dominate
  • Social, Paid Search, and Email are significantly underdeveloped

User Demographics

  • 87%+ of new users are U.S.-based
  • Female users (366) had the highest avg. session time: 146.5 seconds
  • Unknown users averaged only 93.0 seconds — lower engagement
  • 80%+ of age data marked “unknown” due to privacy settings
  • Known users mostly aged 25–44 — strong digital audience

Landing Page Performance

Page New Users
Homepage 486
/crm 394
/ichill 300
/trm 194
/trm-trainings 54
/crm-trainings 33
  • Training pages have low traffic despite high intent
  • Need better SEO and internal linking

Engagement Metrics

  • 9,210 page views — strong content visibility
  • 8,542 engagement events from 2,138 users
  • 3,940 session starts — indicates repeat visits
  • 1,678 scrolls by 949 users — deep content engagement
  • TRM pages avg. 1 min 52 sec vs. 57 sec site average

Key Findings

  • Organic search & direct traffic account for nearly all acquisition
  • TRM-related pages show significantly above-average engagement
  • Email engagement: 2 users, 22.22% rate (benchmark: 66%)
  • Paid Search: only 17 users, 27.27% engagement rate
  • High-impression keywords like “resiliency” had 0% click-through rates

Recommendations

1. Optimize Landing Pages Clear CTAs, trust signals, A/B testing

2. Enhance SEO Rewrite metadata, create dedicated training pages, target long-tail keywords

3. Leverage Google Ads Target high-intent keywords, geo-targeting, retargeting

4. Improve Demographic Tracking Optional surveys, enhanced GA4 tracking

5. Boost Email & Social Monthly newsletter, repurpose top content for LinkedIn & Instagram

Conclusion

TRI’s website showed strong organic performance with a core audience of U.S.-based females aged 25–44.

By focusing on:

  • Improved CTAs and landing pages
  • SEO optimization
  • Paid search campaigns
  • Better demographic tracking

TRI can increase conversions and better promote CRM and TRM training programs.

Challenges

  • Data Gaps: 80%+ of demographic data unknown
  • Conversion Tracking: Unable to analyze full conversion paths
  • Time Constraints: Only 3 months of data available
